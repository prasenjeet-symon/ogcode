The page pulls every order in one call and filters in the browser, so the filters have to move into the query before paging can work. The API already returns a next cursor, so cursor paging fits. Give listOrders a cursor and a limit of 50, move the page to useInfiniteQuery keyed on the filters so a filter change starts again from page one, and show Load more while hasNextPage is true. One test: set status=paid, load page 2 and check the request still carries the filter. Page is already exported from the types, so there is nothing new to declare.

On it. I'll give listOrders a cursor, load 50 rows at a time and carry the active filters through every page.

Before you subscribe.

What $5 a month gets you, answered plainly.

Will I ever pay more than $5?
No. $5 a month is the whole bill, and there is no overage. When a day's budget is spent, OGX simply pauses until the daily refill at 00:00 UTC, or you can borrow ahead from your own cycle. Borrowing is free, and nothing extra is ever charged.
What does “$2 of tokens a day” mean?
It is your daily budget, counted at the models’ own list prices: each call draws its list-price cost from the day’s $2, whichever model you use. That comes to $60 of tokens over a 30-day cycle, and the bill stays $5.
What happens if I use up today's budget?
OGX pauses until the budget refills at 00:00 UTC, and nothing is charged. On a heavy day you can borrow up to three days from the end of your cycle and keep going, and heavy users may also wake up to a surprise boost.
How does borrowing work?
With one tap from your OGLAB dashboard, you can take one, two or three days from the end of your current cycle and add them to today. Each day brings a full day’s budget, so borrow 2 and today holds $6 of tokens instead of $2. There is no extra charge: they are your own days, used early, and your cycle ends that many days lighter. A cycle lends three days in all, and borrowing is available while your cycle has more than three days left.
What’s a surprise boost?
A little thank-you for heavy use. If you use OGX heavily, you may now and then find extra budget added to your day, at no extra cost. We’ll email you when it happens.
Which models are included?
Two: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and GLM 5.3 Flash. Both draw on the same daily budget, and you can mix them however you like. At list prices a GLM call costs more than a DeepSeek call, so it draws more of the day’s $2, and is worth more too.
How fast is it, really?
In our September 2026 measurements (the median of 12 streamed coding prompts per model), DeepSeek V4.1 Flash streamed about 310 tokens a second and GLM 5.3 Flash about 215, and both started answering in about a second. “First token” counts the first streamed token, reasoning included. We measured against the plan's model host, so your own network adds a round trip.
Can I use OGX outside ogcode?
No. OGX is made for ogcode and only works inside it.
Is there a free tier?
No. OGX is $5 a month. ogcode itself stays free and open source.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from the OGLAB dashboard. Cancelling stops future charges and gives up your seat.
What does “seats are limited” mean?
OGX carries a limited number of subscribers at a time. When every seat is taken you can join the waitlist, and you get an email when one opens.
